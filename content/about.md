I'm a Senior Full-Stack Engineer with 10+ years leading production work across healthcare, fintech, e-commerce, and media. I've shipped at every layer of the stack — Vue and React front ends, PHP/Laravel and Node/.NET back ends, AWS and GKE infrastructure, Docker environments, and CI/CD pipelines — and I've earned HIPAA certification on a regulated behavioral-health platform along the way.

The work has ranged from custom CMSs for startups, to eCommerce integrations across Shopify, Magento, and BigCommerce, to enterprise internal tools at Fortune 500 scale. I've led the migration from monolith to services, stood up the first AWS DevOps practice a company ever had, engineered multi-stage Docker dev environments that replaced "works on my machine," and migrated platforms off aging container orchestrators onto modern Kubernetes. I've also taken a stubborn pride in replacing expensive third-party SaaS with in-house builds — one migration saved a client $21,600 in its first year.

Currently **Senior Software Engineer III at Cox Communications**, where I'm the lead engineer on [Jump](/work/01-jump), Cox's internal URL shortener platform. I led the rewrite from Vue 2 to Vue 3 + Vite + Pinia with a team of three, drove the migration off Rancher onto Google Anthos GKE, and built the full test harness — Vitest, `vitest-browser`, Playwright, SonarQube quality gates on every PR — that didn't exist when I joined.
